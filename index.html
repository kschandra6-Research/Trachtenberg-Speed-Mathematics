Prof. Jakow Trachtenberg — Historical Timeline & Institute

1888: Early Prodigy & Imperial Shipyards
Born in Odessa, Russia. Graduated with highest engineering honors from the Imperial Institute. Headed the Obuschoff shipyards directing 11,000 men.
1939–1945: The Camps & Survival Mathematics
Imprisoned in Nazi concentration camps. Deprived of paper, pencil, and books, he preserved his mind and spirit by mentally constructing an entire new system of mathematics with no tables.
1950: The Zurich Mathematical Institute
Following a daring escape to Switzerland orchestrated by his devoted wife, he opened the world-renowned Mathematical Institute in Zurich, transforming failing students into mathematical wizards.
Cognitive & Psychological Revolution
Trachtenberg demonstrated that fear of mathematics is not a lack of innate ability, but a conditioned anxiety born of rote table memorization. His system rebuilds self-confidence and neuro-associative speed.
